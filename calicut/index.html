Rules and tolerances

Rate tolerances are in rupees, not percent. A plan rate of 19 works out to 19 / 1.18 = 16.102, which Tally may print as either 16.10 or 16.11 — the ₹0.02 sq.ft tolerance accepts both. Quantity tolerances are absolute for the same reason: a percentage would let a large-volume line hide a real shortfall.

Item code reference

Used to confirm that a plan row and a bill line are the same product. Seeded only from the CLT bills of 28-Aug-2026 — extend it as you confirm more codes. An unknown code never blocks a match, it just stops the tool vouching for the thickness. Two worth remembering: sheet 2x2 is 65×65mm on the thin grades but 60×60mm on the 7mm, so every 60×60 line gets a badge on the audit; and 1/2x1/2 10g lite bills at 1.9mm here where EKM writes 2.0mm.
